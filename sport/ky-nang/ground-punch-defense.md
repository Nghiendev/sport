## Phân tích tình huống

Ground punch defense là **phòng haymaker/ground strikes** khi **nằm dưới** — street fight hoặc MMA scramble. Mục tiêu: **cover head**, **close distance/clinch**, **reguard**, hoặc **technical stand-up** — không sport guard pull mù.

Khi nào dùng: attacker **mounted or in guard** đang **punch down**; bạn bottom. Thứ tự: **cover** (forearms, absorb) → **hip escape / bridge** → **clinch attacker** (pull down) → **reguard** hoặc [technical stand-up](./technical-stand-up.md) → disengage.

Dấu hiệu nguy hiểm: **multiple strikes**, **head against wall**. Lỗi: **open guard sport** — punch through; **arms wide** blocking body only — head exposed; không **close distance** — punches keep landing.

Self-defense vs sport: **ưu tiên đứng** hoặc **top**; không 50/50 sport games. Cover → stand > submit from bottom in street context.

---

## Cách phòng thủ / hóa giải

*(Góc nhìn người **đang đấm người nằm**)*

- **Posture up** between punches — reset angle.
- **Switch sides** — find openings around cover.
- **Control wrists** after cover — pin arms, mount solid.
- **Sprawl** nếu họ technical stand-up.
- **Clinch knees** when they pull you down into guard.
