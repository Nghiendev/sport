## Phân tích tình huống

**Trọng tâm đai xanh (§16):** [butterfly sweep](./butterfly-sweep.md), [knee slice](./knee-cut-pass.md), [over-under pass](./over-under-pass.md), **open guards** (spider, DLR, butterfly), [chains](./chain-attacks.md), **leg locks cơ bản** ([straight ankle](./straight-ankle-lock.md)).

Mục tiêu: **A-game shape** — guard OR pass identity forming. Thêm [scrambling](./scrambling.md), [guard retention](./guard-retention.md). Competition optional — learn [advantages](./advantages-ibjjf.md).

1–2 năm: depth > breadth — 3 open guards max.

---

## Cách phòng thủ / hóa giải

*(Blue belt plateaus)*

- **Fix holes** — if always passed, retention not new guard.
- **Teaching white belts** solidifies basics.
- **Gi and/or no-gi** choice — §14.
