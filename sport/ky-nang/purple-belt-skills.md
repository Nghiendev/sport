## Phân tích tình huống

**Trọng tâm đai tím (§16):** **Game cá nhân**, [berimbolo](./berimbolo-back-take.md), **leg entanglements** ([50/50](./fifty-fifty-guard.md), [heel hook](./heel-hook.md) where allowed), **phản công** ([counter submission](./counter-submission.md)), **đặc sản** (signature passes/subs).

Mục tiêu: **proven systems** — not random technique collector. Competition strategy [stalling defense](./stalling-defense.md), [chain attacks](./chain-attacks.md). Mentor lower belts.

3–5 năm: refine A-game, add **B-plan** when A stuffed.

---

## Cách phòng thủ / hóa giải

*(Purple expectations)*

- **Counters** to your A-game must exist — study competition.
- **Injury management** — long path to brown.
- **Positional sparring** refine weak links.
