# Drilling vs Rolling

> **Tiếng Anh:** Drilling vs sparring · **50/50 balance**

## Phân tích tình huống

Drilling vs rolling = **cân bằng** — drill **embeds motor pattern**; roll **tests under resistance**. Coach thường gợi ý **50/50** hoặc drill-heavy cho lower belts.

Drill: cooperative reps, **start position**, increase resistance gradually. Roll: **full resistance**, explore. Lỗi chỉ roll: **bad habits reinforced**; chỉ drill: **không apply** live.

White belt: **drill fundamentals** (shrimp, upa, scissor) + **positional roll**. Purple+: more roll, targeted drill weak spots.

§15: 50/50 hoặc theo coach — không dogma, listen gym culture.

---

## Cách phòng thủ / hóa giải

*(Imbalanced gym)*

- **Ask coach** for drill time if only roll gym.
- **Pre-class drill** partner 15 min if needed.
- **Positional sparring** bridge — see [positional sparring](./positional-sparring.md).
