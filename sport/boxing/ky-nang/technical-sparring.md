# Technical sparring

> **Tiếng Anh:** Technical Sparring · [← Quay lại bách khoa](../TAT-CA-KY-NANG-BOXING.md#10-training) · ⭐⭐ · Training / sparring / light

---

## Phân tích tình huống

Technical sparring: light contact (30-50%), focus technique và defense — không score. Often jab only or one combo at a time. Lỗi: escalate to war, không respect light touch.

---

## Cách phòng thủ / hóa giải

Technical spar defense: honor light touch — nếu partner escalate, restate rules hoặc stop. Counter light vẫn OK — teaches timing. Focus designated combos only không wild. Give feedback constructive sau round.
