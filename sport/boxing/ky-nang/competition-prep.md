## Phân tích tình huống

Competition prep: 6-8 week camp — taper volume week before, weight cut plan, study opponent tape, game plan 2-3 strategies. Mock sparring rounds. Lỗi: peak too early, bad weight cut.

---

## Cách phòng thủ / hóa giải

Scout opponent trong prep: họ study you too — vary camp patterns. Hide injury limitations. Game plan B if A fails ring day. Weight cut opponent may weaken — hoặc họ strong nếu cut tốt.
