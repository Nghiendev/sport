# Phòng thủ clinch

> **Tiếng Anh:** Clinch Defense · [← Quay lại bách khoa](../TAT-CA-KY-NANG-BOXING.md#9-clinch) · ⭐⭐ · Clinch / defense / avoid

---

## Phân tích tình huống

Clinch defense: avoid bị tie — hand fight, forearm frame, không let opponent head under chin. Jab push before clinch. Lỗi: accept clinch when losing outside.

---

## Cách phòng thủ / hóa giải

Force clinch on opponent who hates inside: pressure step in on their punch recovery. Tie on jab return. Inside step after body shot. Head under their chin legal position. Ref will break — plan punch on break. Train clinch daily if strategy.
