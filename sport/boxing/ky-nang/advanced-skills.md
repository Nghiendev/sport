## Phân tích tình huống

Advanced: counter punching, ring generalship, inside fighting, check hook, shoulder roll, switch hitting, fight camp ready, amateur/pro competition. Years of dedicated training.

---

## Cách phòng thủ / hóa giải

Vs advanced: few holes — need physical edge game plan discipline. Don't lazy — one mistake costly. Respect và sharp strategy.
