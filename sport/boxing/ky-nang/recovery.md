---

## Phân tích tình huống

Recovery: sleep 8h, hydration, protein, active recovery (light jog, stretch), ice sore areas. Rest days — không train hard 7 ngày/tuần. Lỗi: overtrain, không sleep, fight camp no recovery.

---

## Cách phòng thủ / hóa giải

Opponent overtrain no recovery: they fade round 2–3 — increase pace hiệp sau. Bad weight cut opponent weak round 1 — capitalize early. Your recovery better = sustained output late rounds.
