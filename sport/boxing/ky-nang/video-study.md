## Phân tích tình huống

Video study: xem opponent fights — note patterns, favorite combos, defense holes. Xem own sparring tape sửa form. 30 min/week minimum serious fighters.

---

## Cách phòng thủ / hóa giải

Opponent study you on tape too — show different looks each bout. Spar diverse partners hide patterns. New combos camp không show public sparring.
