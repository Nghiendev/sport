# Partner drills

> **Tiếng Anh:** Partner Drills · [← Quay lại bách khoa](../TAT-CA-KY-NANG-BOXING.md#10-training-luyện-tập) · ⭐ · Training / partner / technique

---

## Phân tích tình huống

Partner drills: slip-bar, parry-counter, jab-only sparring, body sparring — controlled technique work. Communicate intensity. Lỗi: drill become brawl, no gloves/mouthguard.

---

## Cách phòng thủ / hóa giải

Partner drill safety: communicate power level trước. Gloves mouthguard mandatory. Stop nếu partner flinch excessive — giảm speed. Switch offense defense roles equal time. Coach supervise parry-counter drills — timing sensitive.
