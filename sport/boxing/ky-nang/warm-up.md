---

## Phân tích tình huống

Warm-up trước boxing: jump rope 5 min, dynamic stretch (arm circles, hip openers), shadowbox light 2 rounds, gradually increase intensity. Lỗi: không warm-up (injury), static stretch cold muscles.

---

## Cách phòng thủ / hóa giải

Warm-up không trực tiếp defense — nhưng opponent skip warm-up có thể slow round 1. Pressure early test legs. Body shot early if they cold. Respect own warm-up — injury risk nếu skip.
