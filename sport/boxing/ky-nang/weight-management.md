---

## Phân tích tình huống

Weight management: walk-around weight vs fight weight — cut gradual không crash. Weigh-in 24h before (pro) or day-of (amateur tùy). Rehydrate sau weigh-in. Lỗi: cut quá nhiều (performance drop, health risk).

---

## Cách phòng thủ / hóa giải

Opponent bad weight cut: pressure round 1–2 before hydrate recover. Body attack dehydrated opponent. Your proper weight management = performance edge late.
