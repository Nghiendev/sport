# Vùng đích và lỗi

> **Tiếng Anh:** Legal Targets and Fouls · [← Quay lại bách khoa](../TAT-CA-KY-NANG-BOXING.md#11-rules-luật) · ⭐ · Rules / fouls / safety

---

## Phân tích tình huống

Legal targets: front of head, front/side body above belt. Fouls: low blow, headbutt, hit back of head/kidney, hold and hit, rabbit punches. Warning → point deduction → DQ. Groin protector bắt buộc amateur.

---

## Cách phòng thủ / hóa giải

Opponent foul intentionally: talk ref immediately. Protect groin low guard. Don't retaliate foul — point deduction them. Know legal targets — body above belt front side.
