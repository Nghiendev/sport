## Phân tích tình huống

Ego management = **tap = học, không thua** — resisting tap → injury, slower progress. **Lower belt submit you** = họ improve; **you tap** = you safe.

Khi ego cao: **don't tap**, **go 100% vs smaller partner**, **skip fundamentals**, **belt compare**. Hậu quả: **shoulder blown**, **no one rolls you**, **plateau**.

Thực hành: **thank partner** after sub; **ask** what they hit; **celebrate** training streak not tap count. §15 video: belt system, egos about tapping.

Longevity BJJ = **decade game** — ego shortens career.

---

## Cách phòng thủ / hóa giải

*(Culture fix)*

- **Normalize tapping** loud in gym.
- **Coach models** tapping to students.
- **No belittling** after submit.
