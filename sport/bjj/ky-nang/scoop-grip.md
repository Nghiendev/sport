# Scoop Grip (Scoop grip / Underhook sâu)

## Phân tích tình huống

Scoop grip là **underhook sâu** — tay **scoop dưới háng/leg** bottom thay vì chỉ nách — tạo **leverage** cho sweep, pass, hoặc **back take**. Jason Rau favorite từ **De La Riva** và half guard systems.

Khi nào dùng: **DLR guard** bottom scooping top leg; **half guard** top/bottom underhook battles; **passing** when trapping leg. Mechanics: arm **deep under thigh/hip**, shoulder drive, combine **head control** or **collar grip** — off-balance top/bottom tùy context.

Dấu hiệu hiệu quả: đối thủ **base broken**, leg **lifted/trapped**. Lỗi: scoop **shallow** — they pummel out; scoop **without head/post control** — they counter; arm **overextended** — kimura trap.

Chain: scoop grip → **DLR sweep**, **back take**, **leg drag pass**. Liên quan [de la riva guard](./de-la-riva-guard.md), [underhook](./underhook.md).

---

## Cách phòng thủ / hóa giải

*(Góc nhìn người **bị scoop grip**)*

- **Pummel** — win underhook back, [pummeling](./pummeling.md).
- **Post hand on mat** — base when leg scooped.
- **Backstep / clear hook** — DLR context, kill scoop entry.
- **Kimura trap** on scooping arm — counter from half/side.
- **Sprawl leg** — when bottom scoops your leg in half guard pass.
