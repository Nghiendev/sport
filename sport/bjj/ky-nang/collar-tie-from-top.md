## Phân tích tình huống

Collar tie from top là **nắm cổ/gáy bottom** khi bạn ở **pin top** (side, mount, knee on belly) hoặc **passing** — kiểm soát **head direction**, break frames, setup **cross collar choke**, **gift wrap**, hoặc **transition mount**.

Khi nào dùng: gi training hoặc **no-gi neck grip** (legal per rules); bottom **frames neck**. Mechanics: **deep collar grip** or **grip behind neck** → pull head **off center** → combine **crossface/underhook** → break posture for attack.

Dấu hiệu hiệu quả: bottom **không frame hiệu quả**, head **turned away**. Lỗi: collar tie **không có hip control** — they shrimp; **pull up only** — they bridge; illegal **small joint** grips in competition — know rules.

Chain: collar tie → **cross collar choke**, **mount**, **gift wrap**. Liên quan standing [collar tie](./collar-tie.md) — cùng grip, context khác.

---

## Cách phòng thủ / hóa giải

*(Góc nhìn người **bị collar tie top**)*

- **Strong frames** — forearms block collar grip before deep.
- **Shrimp away from tied side** — create angle break grip.
- **Two-on-one peel** — break collar grip.
- **Bridge + turn in** — when tie overcommitted.
- **Posture up** (from bottom guard context) — break tie with hip escape.
