## Phân tích tình huống

**Trọng tâm nâu/đen (§16):** **Tinh chỉnh**, **counters**, **coaching**, [pin pressure](./pin-pressure.md), **efficiency** — win with less movement, micro-adjustments.

Brown: polish competition game, teach formally. Black: **curriculum**, gym culture, [good training partner](./good-training-partner.md) modeling.

Top 10 competition subs §16 (RNC, guillotine, armbar, arm triangle, triangle, darce, kimura, anaconda, kneebar, heel hook) — **teach và counter** tất cả.

Efficiency = [pacing](./pacing-bjj.md) + precision — không cần prove athleticism mỗi roll.

---

## Cách phòng thủ / hóa giải

*(Staying sharp at high belt)*

- **Learn from lower belts** — new games appear.
- **Film competition** — micro errors visible.
- **Physical maintenance** — mobility, not only roll hard.
